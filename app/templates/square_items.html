Square items

What each item you sell through Square means for a tournament's takings, matched by its name. Payments are split by the line items of the order rung up for them: venue fees, bracket fees and catch-ups (a player paying for a week they missed: it counts toward the week it was paid at) count toward the takings; anything that's not a fee, like drinks or food, is left out. Items without a meaning yet still count, as they did before.
